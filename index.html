02 / ABOUT

How I got here

My path into data engineering began as a Business Intelligence Analyst. The business had invoices, purchase orders, sales records, and spreadsheets, but no established database or warehouse. I helped organise those records so we could analyse sales, stock, and customer balances.

I liked the work behind the report: deciding what each row meant, defining consistent measures, and finding out why two records didn’t agree. Through my own projects, I started taking responsibility for more of that process, from collecting the data to deploying and running the pipelines.

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ANALYST

Sales, inventory & reconciliation

A customer with several branches needed two views: each branch on its own and the customer as a whole. I worked on the models and measures behind those reports, including year-on-year sales and product demand.

INDEPENDENT PROJECTS / DATA PLATFORMS

Infrastructure, pipelines & deployment

The London project taught me to separate daily source collection from the server that processes and displays the data. In the financial-data pipeline, I focused on corrections and backfills: what should happen when the same record arrives again with a different value?

I’m now extending that work through Lakebase Commerce, Northbridge, and FEMA. They cover different domains, but each gives me a reason to work through the details of how data is captured, checked, and used.

CURRENT WORK / IN PROGRESS

FEMA Claims Intelligence

I’m building a local-first claims pipeline using public FEMA data and synthetic documents. It validates records, extracts information from PDFs and emails, and flags discrepancies with source evidence. Ollama extraction and retrieval-based question answering are in progress; containerised batch deployment and a human review interface are planned.
